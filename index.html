Settings

How hits are logged, and how your daily limit is measured.

Daily limit

When on, grams you don’t use each day are added to the next day’s allowance (capped at 2× your daily limit). The Today card shows this as “carried over”.

Since you don't know your hit count yet, the app estimates each hit at this weight. Adjust it as you learn your real per-hit amount. You can override per hit in “Add details”.

Sleep window

Time inside this window is skipped by "Since last", "Longest gap" and the average gap between hits, so a normal night doesn't read as a nine-hour break. Your hits are never changed.

Per-device grams per hit — optional

Override the global default for specific devices. Leave a field blank to use the global default above.

Bust-up

This is the amount the “• Bust up” button logs and marks on your calendar. Change it whenever you like — 1g on a light day, 3g on a heavier one. Bust-ups you already logged keep the amount they were recorded with.

Privacy & security

The PIN gates the app UI. On its own it is basic privacy against casual viewing — turn on encryption below to actually protect the stored data.

Encryption at rest

Encrypted with AES-256-GCM; the key is derived from your PIN (PBKDF2, 210k rounds) and never stored. A forgotten PIN cannot be reset — the data is unrecoverable, so keep a backup.

Data & backups

Your data lives only in this browser. It is mirrored to IndexedDB, but that copy dies with the browser's site data — so keep a file backup off-device (cloud drive, USB).
